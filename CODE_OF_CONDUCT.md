# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant v2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful, assume good faith, give and accept constructive feedback gracefully,
and keep discussion focused on the work. Harassment or discrimination of any kind is not tolerated.

To report a problem, use GitHub's private
[security advisory form](https://github.com/KomaliG7/AutoFixAI/security/advisories/new) or contact the
maintainer through their GitHub profile. Reports are handled privately.
